--About--

In today’s world, we generate waste and consume resources without always realizing the impact. Digital Alchemist transforms everyday habits — like food waste, water use, and electricity consumption — into powerful digital elements.

As the theme is digital alchemy, through a system of runes, eco-scores, and rewards, users transmute raw data into long term progress. By turning sustainability into a game, we make small daily actions feel meaningful — and powerful.

Here is a game to promote sustainability
